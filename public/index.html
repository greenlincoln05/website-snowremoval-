Plattsburgh sidewalk snow rules

The 24-hour rule

The City of Plattsburgh requires owners of property that borders a sidewalk to clear snow and ice within 24 hours after a snow event ends. If it isn't cleared, the city can remove it and bill the owner, and a fine is possible.

Read the City of Plattsburgh snow and ice procedures
